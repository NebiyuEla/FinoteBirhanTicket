# FinoteBirhan v1.3.6 accounting hardening

- Revenue is the canonical package-price sum of paid purchases. Bundle is always 300 ETB.
- Admin dashboard shows total sales value, direct sales, seller-entered sales, and package breakdown.
- Seller-originated sales always settle to FinoteBirhan; seller-owned destinations are ignored for new sales.
- Sellers cannot mark their own sales paid/SOLD. FinoteBirhan admin approval is required.
- Existing historical payment-target fields remain for audit, but are no longer presented as proof of money received.
